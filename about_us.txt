WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OILS-3 (Oops, I Learnt Something!) is an open-source, interactive terminal lab 
companion created to help engineering students genuinely understand and master 
their practical curriculum.

Targeted at 3rd Semester Computer Science & Engineering students under the VTU 
2025 Scheme, OILS-3 covers Data Structures (Theory: 1BCS305 / Lab: 1BCSL306), Java OOP (1BCS302), and 
Git Version Control (1BCSL307A). Instead of copying programs for manual marks, 
students can interact with step-by-step memory models, read concise block-by-block 
logic breakdowns, resolve emergency debugging bugs, and practice timed viva drills 
with dynamically randomized questions.


HOW THIS STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The idea originated from PRAJWAL D CHONNAD — currently an intern at Vajravyuha 
and a B.Tech Computer Science student. Being deeply passionate about music 
production and DJing, Prajwal experienced firsthand how difficult and counter-
intuitive it was to learn programming languages through conventional, rote-heavy 
teaching methods. He envisioned an interactive, hands-on tool that makes learning 
code intuitive rather than stressful.

To bring the project to life, he collaborated with ADITYA GOTHE, his classmate 
and the Founder of Vajravyuha.in. Aditya organized the core idea, restructured 
the design, and engineered the modular terminal architecture to turn it into a 
reliable, full-featured lab companion.
